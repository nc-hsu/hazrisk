# Citing hazrisk

If you use hazrisk in your work, please cite the software, giving the version
you used. The citation details are in
[`CITATION.cff`](https://github.com/nc-hsu/hazrisk/blob/main/CITATION.cff); the
"Cite this repository" button on GitHub formats them for you.

Please also cite the methods you used:

- the first-order closed forms: Cornell et al. (2002);
- the second-order hazard approximation and its closed forms: Vamvatsikos
  (2013);
- the PCHIP interpolation (scipy's `PchipInterpolator`): Fritsch & Butland
  (1984) and Moler (2004);
- the Poisson relations between probability, frequency and return period, and
  the risk integral: Baker, Bradley & Stafford (2021).

The full references are on the {doc}`references` page. If you use the example
hazard curves, please cite ESHM20 (Danciu et al. 2021), as described on the
{doc}`data` page.
